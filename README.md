## 🔬 About Me

I specialize in the convergence of **Multimedia Communications**, **6G Infrastructure**, and **Data Spaces**. My R&D focus is on building advanced application-network synergies, allowing immersive multimedia systems and next-generation 6G architectures to operate seamlessly as an intelligent, unified system.

* 💼 **30+ R&D Projects** managed across national and international consortia.
* 📝 **20+ Scientific Publications** bridging research and industrial deployment.
* 🌱 **Upstream Contributor** to open-source multimedia processing frameworks.

---

## 🚀 Core Research Areas & Expertise

* 🎥 **Multimedia Communications:** Maximizing Quality of Experience (QoE) via advanced encoding and low-latency streaming protocols *(WebRTC, DASH/CMAF, MoQ/QUIC)*.
* 📡 **6G & Edge Orchestration:** Designing "Network-Application Synergy" using standardized Network APIs *(3GPP NWDAF/NEF, GSMA CAMARA)* for dynamic resource orchestration across RAN and Edge.
* 🔐 **Data Spaces:** Developing secure, sovereign, and decentralized data-sharing frameworks to ensure interoperability and data ownership between consumers and producers across distributed 6G environments.
* 🤖 **Agentic AI & Trusted Access:** Leveraging Distributed Ledger Technologies (DLT) to establish transparency and immutable trust, enabling autonomous agents with policy-governed access to sensitive data and network resources.
