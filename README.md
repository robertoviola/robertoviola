# Hi, I'm Roberto Viola 👋

### 🔬 Senior Researcher at Vicomtech | PhD in ICT
I specialize in the convergence of **Multimedia Communications**, **6G Infrastructure**, and **Data Spaces**. My research focuses on architecting network-application synergies, allowing next-generation multimedia applications and 6G networks to operate seamlessly as an intelligent, unified system.

---

### 🚀 Core Research Areas & Expertise

*   **Multimedia Communications:** Maximizing Quality of Experience (QoE) via advanced encoding and low-latency streaming protocols *(WebRTC, DASH/CMAF, MoQ/QUIC)*.
*   **6G & Edge Orchestration:** Designing "Network-Application Synergy" using standardized Network APIs *(3GPP NWDAF/NEF, GSMA CAMARA)* for dynamic resource orchestration across RAN and Edge.
*   **Data Spaces:** Developing secure, sovereign, and decentralized data-sharing frameworks to ensure interoperability and data ownership.
*   **Agentic AI & Trusted Access:** Leveraging Distributed Ledger Technologies (DLT) to establish immutable trust and policy-governed resource access for autonomous agents.
